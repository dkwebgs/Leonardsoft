SEO Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our SEO services

How long does it take to see results from SEO?

SEO is a long-term strategy, and it typically takes 3-6 months to start seeing significant results. However, some improvements can be seen within the first month, especially with technical SEO fixes. The timeline depends on various factors including website age, competition, and the current state of your SEO.

Do you guarantee first page rankings?

While we cannot guarantee specific rankings due to the constantly changing nature of search algorithms, we do guarantee that we will implement proven SEO strategies that have a history of improving rankings. We focus on sustainable growth and measurable improvements in organic traffic and conversions.

What's the difference between SEO and SEM?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) focuses on improving your website's visibility in organic (non-paid) search results. SEM (Search Engine Marketing) encompasses both SEO and PPC (Pay-Per-Click) advertising. While SEO provides long-term sustainable results, SEM can deliver immediate visibility through paid ads.

How often will I receive reports on my SEO progress?

We provide monthly comprehensive reports that detail key performance indicators, including keyword rankings, organic traffic, conversions, and other relevant metrics. For our higher-tier plans, we offer more frequent reporting. We also schedule regular calls to discuss your progress and strategy.
